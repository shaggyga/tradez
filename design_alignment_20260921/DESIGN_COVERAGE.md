## How to read the status

- **implemented** means the named bounded behavior was directly established; it does not mean its enclosing design gate passed.
- **partial** means a concrete predecessor/implementation/evidence exists, but integration, scope or acceptance remains incomplete.
- **unverified** means this review did not establish the required behavior. It is not a failed test and is not proof the capability is absent project-wide.
- **absent** is used only for a specifically inspected missing location or artifact, not for an unsuccessful broad search.
- **deferred** means intentionally outside the currently authorized implementation stage.

The design calls for a trustworthy small all-pair slice early, with broader lineage/macro work continuing in parallel. It does not justify another broad rewrite, a one-day ceiling, or a model sweep before chronology/economics are trustworthy. GPT/advisor comparison remains deferred by the user; that deferral does not remove macro, causal feature or management work.

1. **Bind the current stage.** Preserve retained negative artifacts, stale-document history and current user changes. Record effective source/config/input/target identities and correct only current navigation claims. Every pending change should name its R/WP/TST mapping and predecessor; no unreviewed legacy launcher activation.
2. **Repair information and target contracts first.** Decision-origin population must be known at decision time. Apply label maturity to fitting/scoring without dropping future-missing origins. Use chronological inner selection and explicit model-ready fallback. Define elapsed 24h, daily close and counted sessions separately. Publish forecast-only records and separate outcomes.
