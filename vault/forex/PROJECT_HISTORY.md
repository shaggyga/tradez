# How the Forex project developed

Reconstructed September 14, 2026 from retained chats, project logs, vault evidence and source registers. Dates describe records, not a claim of uninterrupted operation.

## Original intent and branches

The recurring objective was a live rolling feature space across 68 pairs, mapped to both observed and subsequent movements, with a next-hour curve to help manage positions. Official releases for 21 currencies were to inform relative currency strength; technical information would help timing and entries/exits. Separate practice accounts were test spaces, not a missing prerequisite.

1. **Early feature/curve work:** several overlapping catalogues and implementations appeared: hundreds of technical/moment inputs, 60-minute curves, currency factors and univariate libraries. Some magnitude results looked useful. Direction and costed performance often failed; some good-looking early results were later invalidated for timing.
2. **Broader model and news experiments:** gradient boosting, ARIMA/SARIMAX, second-ridge horizon heads, peer/currency inputs, official-release mappings, news/blurb attribution and combined models were already tried. The original 29 INTRA/NEWS/TAG actions remain part of the history.
3. **September 5–9 audit and repairs:** causal clocks, exact endpoints, probability/score meaning, evidence lineage and source recovery received substantial repair. The contemporaneous H1 studies were distinct from the older wide next-hour curve. Six September 9 practice losses were reviewed separately from research metrics.
4. **September 10–12 reconstruction:** five chats were indexed; source/model variants and archive populations were reconciled. The defined 300-source census and 29,366-run catalogue established reuse and recovery dispositions. The 53.5-million-row primary archive supported completed compact/peer comparisons and exact saved-output replay.
5. **September 12–14 buildout:** richer-feature comparisons, typed outcomes, news transport, neutral observation archives and forward evaluation were developed. Some tests and local comparisons passed, but actual simultaneous runtime admission, all-pair feature coverage, shared rolling data and management integration remained incomplete.
6. **September 14 reorientation:** the user paused project work while allowing collection. The later audit confirmed that the gap was both engineering/data plumbing and unproven model value. This vault update organizes that evidence; it does not resume implementation.
