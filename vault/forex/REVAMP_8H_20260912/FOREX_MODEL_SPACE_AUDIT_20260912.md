## What prior performance supports

Direction has sometimes been modestly better than a simple comparator; dependable net returns have not been established. Predicting a large move, predicting its sign, winning after spread, and choosing a profitable entry/exit are different tasks. Percentage accuracy cannot itself be negative; negative return or negative improvement versus a baseline has a different meaning.

The corrected older H60 cross-currency-factor discovery recorded+0.308173bps per selected position in its final scored period, with50.83% direction accuracy and rankIC0.02537. Its subsequent robustness review found HACt0.890, month/session instability and concentration in USD_HUF; it was classified as weak carried discovery, not deployable. Its exact fitted family columns and source bodies remain unrecovered. This is useful prior evidence to preserve, not a reason to call every older model worthless or to deploy that row as a winner.

The broad66-pair returns-only curve showed only0.004770% MAE improvement over zero change: an absolute reduction of about0.000196bps. Other retained curve runs were often worse; isolated percentages on very small or unrecovered sample counts do not establish a best setup. A separate68-pair lagged-correlation network's final top8 net was−0.657761bps per position;22 combined-feature tests also failed to establish positive direction. Those conjunctions were already tried.

The September8 frozen original-outcome baseline reconciled12034 records. Joint-v2's matched2779-decision comparison showed52.07% direction versus50.20% for separately fitted price-only, with mean after-spread−2.9693 versus−3.1702bps. That+0.2009bps sample difference remains negative in absolute terms and comes from dependent pair/hour observations. The September11 fixed108-field comparison likewise found all32 learned arm/horizon means negative after bid/ask endpoint costs. These studies support targeted incremental tests, not the assertion that there is no relationship at all or that adding more fields guarantees improvement.

Gradient boosting, random/extra trees, linear models and several specialist/foundation families already exist in the catalogue.
