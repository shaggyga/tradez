# Currency projection policy comparison

`currency_projection_policy_comparison_v2` is complete within its offline engineering scope. It replays the qualified saved native packets for direct, full currency-projection and half-residual forecasts under unchanged six-arm policy, accounting, cost, risk and dated candle assumptions.

The matrix contains 48 runs: two base methods × three projection variants × two cohorts × two cost scenarios × reference/optimized engines. All 48 completed and the engine-neutral decisions, policy state, event ledger, final state and accounting audit payloads are byte-identical for every engine pair. Each accounting audit is verified and each run has 54 decisions.

No base or layer model was fit or loaded; no API, advisor, broker, service, account or live action occurred. Output is descriptive offline evidence for two overlapping inspected development cohorts. It does not select a forecast winner, establish profitability, confirm forecasting skill, or authorize a demo/live system.
