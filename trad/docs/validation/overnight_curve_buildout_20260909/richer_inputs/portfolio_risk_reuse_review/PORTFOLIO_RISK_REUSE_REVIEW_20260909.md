# Portfolio, co-movement and management reuse review — September 9, 2026

There is already meaningful position management and currency-overlap protection. The useful next step is to connect its verified primitives to an explicit, valued portfolio-state diagnostic before expanding the current paper manager beyond one pair. A new forecasting or broker-execution module is unnecessary for that first step.

This is a bounded read-only source/configuration and retained-evidence audit. No tests, fits, broker requests or runtime changes were performed. The companion JSON binds the exact files and verifies the three current registry closures.

The three inspected registry closures contain none of the new cross-window, risk-label or signed-exposure helper modules. The feature dictionary already describes correlation-cluster exposure as design intent (`docs/FOREX_FEATURE_DICTIONARY_CURRENT.md:10094`); the pending queue and `FOREX_COMOVEMENT_RESEARCH_PLAN_20260906.json` retain matched peer, error-covariance and dependence-aware evaluation work. We should not report these intended fields as active risk controls.

The smallest valuable addition is a **pure decision-time exposure projection** over observed position state and a proposed candidate. Reuse strict currency identity and the existing exact USD conversion/quote rules. Report gross long/short and net currency factors, current-versus-entry valuation convention, each quote's availability, and before/after concentration. Keep the two economic legs separate from account cash and avoid counting both as portfolio gross notional. Refuse missing conversions or malformed positions rather than treating them as zero exposure. First acceptance cases should include unequal offsetting holdings, inverse pairs, gross risk despite net cancellation, three USD-sharing pairs, stale/future conversions and no-order guarantees. This proposal changes no live policy or limit.

Predictor agreement belongs in a separate diagnostic beside that book: match original pair/origin/horizon records and distinguish prediction correlation, realized-return correlation, completed-error covariance and shared event/currency identity. Reuse the historical graph alignment and factor-episode logic after enforcing actual source availability, duplicate and gap rules. Display pairwise support and distinct sessions. Do not treat a graph edge, three agreeing models or two shared-USD trades as independent confirmation. A covariance-based sizing policy needs this additional evidence; deterministic exposure accounting can be built first.

The new direction-neutral risk result supports further range/risk development, not directional alpha. On the already-inspected historical development period, volatility-scaled empirical quantiles improved terminal absolute-move, range and variation pinball loss in all nine pair/horizon cells. Adverse-excursion improvement was mixed: 3/9 long and 4/9 short cells for both close and envelope versions. Each cell has 1,638–1,872 overlapping origins across only nine dates, so no independent-N or confidence-interval claim is available. USD/JPY's 60-minute absolute interval increased coverage from about 72.6% to 76.6% by widening from 11.57 to 18.14 bps, still below its nominal 80%. EUR/USD's 15-minute short-adverse upper-tail miss rate worsened from 7.81% to 11.11%.

For sizing, this warrants a separately frozen, capped inverse-tail development comparison with side-specific risk and observed costs, not larger units now. For holding, retain native 15/30/60-minute targets and compare movement against costs without silently retargeting an issued curve. For stops, future OHLC envelopes do not identify barrier order, slippage, tradeable fills or a safe stop distance. Either-side cost opportunity does not choose the profitable direction. None of these new risk quantiles is consumed by the current registered model or paper manager.
