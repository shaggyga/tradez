Proposed first milestone: version strict labels and evaluate fixed risk baselines on the existing immutable three-pair M1 captures at 15/30/60 minutes. Retain absolute endpoint return, realized path variation, both-side MFE/MAE, and either-side endpoint opportunity as distinct labels. Use the completed origin close, exclude that bar’s earlier high/low, preserve the exact native target, and require complete subsequent real bars for path labels. Keep actual bid/ask missingness explicit. A bar that contains both an adverse and favorable barrier cannot establish their order or an executable fill.

Begin with train-only empirical quantiles and past-volatility-scaled distributions. Predeclare 10/50/90% quantiles, then compare at most one compact risk learner if the baseline/labels pass integrity checks. Evaluate pinball loss, interval width and empirical coverage, tail underestimation and magnitude error on matched origins. Opportunity probabilities require an actual fitted and separately calibrated model; use training-prior Brier and prevalence, not fabricated probabilities. Preserve chronological purge, date-level dependence and all missingness denominators.
