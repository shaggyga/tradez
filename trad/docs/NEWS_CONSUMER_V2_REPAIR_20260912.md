# Separate news consumers with source and classification clocks

Three new inactive modules support the reviewed news-source lineage: `oanda_local_news_sentiment_repair_v2.py`, `oanda_news_topic_identity_reconciliation_v2.py`, and `oanda_entry_news_feature_join_v2.py`.

The repaired current-news producer retains exact article payload bytes and table source identity from a bounded read-only SQLite snapshot, rejecting a disagreement in source ID or kind. It retains the existing relevant-publication 24-hour window, at most 4,096 rows, rather than claiming to represent the full news archive. Its source closure includes the collector, both guard generations, both observation-ledger helpers, classification/collector contracts and tagger. Topic reconciliation requires the new guard cohort and preserves original members and source/classification clocks. The entry diagnostic uses classification availability to decide when a derived signal may be used, and original story/body availability for age and expiry.

Versioned outputs are `local_news_sentiment_repair_v2/current_news_v2.json` and `heartbeat_v2.json`. Earlier readers, source pins, receipts and output defaults retain their original versions. Existing forecast studies and worker configurations have not been repointed to v2. The entry join remains eight diagnostic news fields with unavailable/empty/measured distinctions; this change creates no new fitted model or calibrated probability.

The staged suite passed 197 synthetic checks across the three consumers, the full guard fixture suite and new source/derived-clock cases. It includes the actual classifier, a temporary full-schema SQLite observation ledger and read-only snapshot capture. Root inspected the full version diffs and source/age/identity boundaries. Canonical test evidence is recorded separately in the linked integration workpack. Counts overlap earlier guard tests and do not establish predictive performance.

The untouched legacy collector suite was separately accounted for: 496 cases per version, with 491 passing before core008 and 466 passing after it under the isolated harness. Twenty-five additional old fixtures fail original-source admission before reaching their old downstream assertions; five shared cases remain limited by the isolation context. These are recorded as fixture-migration debt pending explicit current-contract tests, not dismissed as proven harmless.

This is not a live readiness claim. No real database was migrated or captured, and no collector, service or trading account was activated. Remaining boundaries include source-record authentication beyond retained payload assertions, exact SQLite snapshot establishment time, legacy caller migration, current worker/profile binding, publication/readback durability and prospective performance evidence.
