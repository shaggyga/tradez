# Ensemble calibration, direction and caller consistency repair

The old evaluator applied fitted probability calibration to fold scores, but its final bundle refitted raw models without saving those calibrators. The manager then compared raw scores against thresholds selected on calibrated values. It also omitted the saved direction/allocation/agreement rules. Eight original synthetic counterexamples are retained; they establish source defects, not how much any historical account lost because of them.

A new pure `oanda_ensemble_probability_contract_v2.py` retains each event model, calibrator and positive-class mapping, plus a separate conditional direction head when required. It requires explicitly separate mature training/calibration partitions and source/label identities. The same calibrated transformation supplies research evaluation and intake. Event probability, long-allocation score and agreement are named separately; they are not claimed to be calibrated midpoint-up probabilities or a magnitude/stop curve.

The old final-fit entrypoint now refuses inputs that lack those calibration/maturity records. Its CLI refuses `--write-final-bundle` before member/data access. The manager's ensemble intake requires one caller-bound v2 feature snapshot and uses the pure research scorer. An old `shadow_approved` flag is insufficient for the scout fallback; literal signed-entry qualification is also required, and this development contract never grants it. Independently approved production results and all other manager method bodies are preserved.

Independent review found three additional consistency gaps in the first staged helper: mismatched outer bundle metadata, omitted inference-time subset/momentum checks, and unchecked/truncated score arrays. Candidate003 closes the 21 reproduced refusal variations, verifies member counts and identities, and reconstructs aggregate scores/direction from retained member evidence. These are internal consistency checks, not authentication of arbitrary estimator bytes or asserted source records.

The reviewed stage passed 116 synthetic checks: the original 76, 23 independent probes and 17 further edge cases. Seventeen warnings originate from a synthetic in-memory Joblib roundtrip. Root inspected the original path, full helper, both caller patches and the final narrow consistency diff. Canonical tests and source readback are retained separately. The full real manager was not imported or started in these tests; only the changed method bodies were executed with fake self/config and external-action tripwires.

No real models were fitted, old thresholds/artifacts converted, historical reports rewritten, account accessed or runtime activated. The first v2 API accepts the all-instruments subset and the explicitly mapped terminal-profit/onset families. Other subset/curve/major/lead targets require their own adapters. Artifact-byte/source attestation, fit resource limits, actual publication clocks, corresponding decision-level evaluation and profitable-entry evidence remain open. Earlier ATR-multiple summaries mislabeled as pips and the distinct shared-feed side-profit/direction-probability issue are not repaired by this change.
