Added bounded Windows status-write retries and durable sanitized failure details. Original policy math and claim accounting remain unchanged.

The HGB artifact loaded and produced valid synthetic inference after extraction. Its NumPy deprecation warnings are retained; its output is an opportunity measure, not a directional trade signal.

The pure management component uses the existing cost/direction rules. Its input adapter currently covers EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY. Optional missing risk does not invalidate a usable curve; partial reduction and conditional remaining-risk modelling remain unsupported here.

Independent review found unchanged numerical, timing, cost and evaluation settings apart from the explicit successor identities, worker source bindings and preparation metadata. No old forecasts or databases were imported.

The current trial still counts its original durable claims; no historical capacity was refunded or reset.
