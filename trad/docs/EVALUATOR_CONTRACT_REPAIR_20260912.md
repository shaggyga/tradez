# Research evaluator repair — September 12, 2026

The model-gap market evaluator now pools gross winning and losing pips when reporting aggregate profit factor. Previously it averaged cell ratios, which can give a different result: cells with gains/losses of 10/1 and 1/5 produced 5.1 instead of the pooled 11/6. A cell with positive gains and no losses previously received zero profit factor and could fail threshold selection incorrectly.

The corrected contract records gross winning and losing pips and an explicit status. Positive gains with no losses have a null numeric ratio and `no_losses` status. They satisfy a finite profit-factor threshold; the other validation gates still apply. JSON output remains finite. Missing sufficient statistics stay unavailable. A row claiming the new contract cannot silently fall back to a legacy numeric score when its required components are missing.

An aggregate median cannot be recovered by averaging cell medians. Portfolio drawdown cannot be recovered by taking the worst individual cell drawdown. Those aggregate statistics now remain unavailable without pooled outcomes or an ordered portfolio path. The largest individual cell drawdown is retained under its own name.

The unified report and post-gap execution research pipeline carry the corrected metric/status contract. Finite legacy ratios remain labeled legacy; old result files and model scores have not been rewritten. The change does not establish that any historical losing model becomes profitable, and no affected-run count has yet been established. Detailed older reports on D remain unavailable under the current disk-read stop.

Validation: 40 isolated integration tests passed against the actual staged modules, including existing project tests, flat/package import bindings, zero-loss and zero-return boundaries, pooled ratios, malformed/missing statistics, unavailable-status propagation, and nonfinite input rejection. The first harness attempt failed during collection because its package overlay was incomplete; that failure was preserved. Canonical integration verified all original source hashes before writing and all resulting hashes afterward.
