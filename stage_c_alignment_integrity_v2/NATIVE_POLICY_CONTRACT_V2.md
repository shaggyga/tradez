# Native common-target policy-input qualification

This package connects the preserved native curve contract and management adapter
to the existing PolicyReplay and event ledger. It adds no pricing or fill engine.
The input tier is synthetic_fresh_conditional_native_curve.v1, not historical
market evidence. The fixture simulates prospective issue/publication/consumption
clocks through the predecessor's current_research schema; those receipts are
synthetic declarations, not observed live or historical publications.

Every decision retains the entire original curve, publication and consumption
chain, expected source bindings, model/input identities and explicit signed
remaining-financing scenario. The adapter requires a fresh conditional-terminal
expectation declared at the reference information clock, at most2 seconds old.
Native target equality is exact; no interpolation, elapsed-horizon substitution
or subtraction of realized move from an old expectation is admitted. A legacy
curve without that conditioning contract is refused. These checks establish
engineering consistency of the fixture, not the truth or quality of a model.

The original reviewed curve adapter remains unchanged. Its terminal and probability
evidence is retained; zero and negative remaining estimates remain distinct from
missing forecasts. Additional admission fields have their own candidate hash and
retain the predecessor adapter's original candidate hash separately. The policy
recomputes admission from the original packet before creating ledger events, so a
post-admission candidate edit cannot reach accounting. Duplicate pair candidates,
stale information, future consumption and wrong targets produce explicit refusals.

The existing policy controller accepts native curve candidates alongside its
preserved momentum mode. Cash, fixed hold, naive, continuation and hysteresis use
the same executable synthetic quote, costs, financing and common target. Recovered
legacy rotation requires momentum confidence/score fields that native curves do
not provide; that arm explicitly abstains. No confidence or calibrated probability
is invented to manufacture a six-way matched comparison. Risk/deadline exits still
take precedence. Partial replacement waits for actual exit fills and a new decision.

The native frozen operator validates source, predecessor and environment identities
before local runtime imports, runs reference and optimized engines, requires ten
admitted native candidates and actual entry/exit coverage, checks every event with
the existing scalar oracle and verifies byte parity. Existing policy runner frame
checkpoints provide real crash recovery. The expanded portable accounting checkpoint
includes all four synthetic recipes and the exact two native predecessor modules.

Historical policy remains blocked. Required next inputs are actual freshly conditioned
remaining-horizon forecasts for original thesis targets plus separately qualified
execution/conversion/financing and arrival assumptions. The completed historical
gross-midpoint forecast slice remains preserved and inadmissible for this purpose.
Full campaign, independent review, promotion and trading readiness remain open.
