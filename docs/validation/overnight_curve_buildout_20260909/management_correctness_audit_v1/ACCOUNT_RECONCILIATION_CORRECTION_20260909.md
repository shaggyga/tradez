# Existing account-manager reconciliation correction

The technical and advisor account-manager sources now distinguish confirmed open, confirmed closed, and unknown broker observations. This is a tested source correction in two older account lanes, with no account-manager import, process start, broker request, or activation during this work. The joint20, curve-pilot14, observed-paper20 and prospective-risk8 registered source sets remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

The original AST-only diagnostics reproduced four reachable defects in both lanes: failed or disabled rechecks could be treated as closed; an explicit missing trade ID could fall back to another trade; a partial close could report the requested quantity after a different observed reduction; and general TIGHTEN could send a weaker stop. The original sources and diagnostic failures remain preserved. Passing those20 original diagnostic cases means the defects were reproduced, not that the old behavior was accepted.

The new pure `oanda_trade_reconciliation_v1.py` checks complete trade-list shape, exact identity, signed units, state transitions and protective prices. Both managers use its typed observation at close reconciliation and at enabled entry rechecks. A failed, disabled, malformed, ambiguous or conflicting recheck is unknown. It cannot imply zero remaining units, log a successful close quantity, complete action deduplication, or authorize a real FLIP entry. Explicit IDs never fall back to another ID; conflicting supplied instruments are refused before selecting a different pair.

Full and partial close responses now require an independently observed exact requested reduction on the same ID, instrument, side and baseline. Requested and observed units remain separate. This confirms position state only: it does not attribute the reduction to a particular submitted order. Partial-percentage settings and integer-unit rounding remain unchanged; fractional-unit partial reduction requires a separate contract. Existing later-cycle retries remain unchanged, with no immediate blind resubmission of an ambiguous close.

TIGHTEN rechecks the actual trade and matching instrument metadata, then checks the original broker quote timestamp and explicit tradeability. Quotes must be no more than30seconds old; missing, invalid, future or stale timestamps are refused, never replaced with local receipt time. Rounded proposed prices cannot weaken an existing valid fixed, guaranteed or known trailing stop. Missing existing trailing-stop level is unknown. Existing transport price formatting remains unchanged. A successful dependent-order response remains transport acceptance, not independently observed execution.

The technical weekend summary now counts only confirmed accepted closes and records dry runs separately. Local-monitor emergency labels remain descriptions of attempted actions; the typed action log supplies the actual confirmation status. The configured open-entry recheck opt-out is preserved deliberately: disabling it retains the old initial-entry policy, but a disabled close recheck still cannot authorize a real FLIP.

Validation: 86 focused tests passed in 36.23 seconds. Tests compile only selected AST-extracted manager methods and use in-memory fake transport; they also exercise the new pure helper. They cover ambiguous close responses, disabled and failed rechecks, exact IDs and supplied instruments, signed partial reductions, full-close quantities under low ambient Decimal precision, protective long/short prices and original quote clocks, existing trailing protection, malformed complete responses, and caller success counters. Fixed 192-digit arithmetic prevents caller Decimal settings from changing reconciliation or protection checks.
