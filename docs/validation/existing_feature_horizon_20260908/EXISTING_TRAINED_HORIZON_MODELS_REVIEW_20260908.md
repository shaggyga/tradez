# Existing trained horizon models — bounded audit, 8 September 2026

**The project already contains broad trained forecast curves and saved models.** Describing its capability only through the newly registered H1 study is incomplete. Existing work includes an eight-endpoint, 795-numeric-feature unified model; a 643-feature moving-average grid; 884 second-ridge pair/horizon parameter surfaces; and actual saved 200-plus-feature direction and movement classifiers. The practical questions are which versions/data are compatible, what their validation really measured, and which components can be reused without inheriting known defects.

This review read source, bounded JSON/Markdown metadata and opaque model bytes. It did not load serialized Python objects, open databases, train, rescore, start processes or change project files. D-drive archives and the retained AppData research directory were inspected because the canonical C project and vault explicitly reference them. Exact paths, copies and hashes are in `EXISTING_CURVE_MODEL_EVIDENCE_20260908.json`.

## What the retained scores establish

**Unified795:** validation-only selection chose `extra_trees_mtf_full`. The report retains a 60-minute purge and 126,415 training / 39,652 validation / 38,962 final rows; the selected candidate used an 80,000-row training cap. Final mean direction accuracy across eight horizons was **49.416%**. Mean horizon MAE relative to predicting no change was **1.000914**: essentially the no-change baseline, slightly worse. The report's forecast and after-cost gates both failed.

Its top-one-pair-per-timestamp/horizon diagnostic reports −180.660 native pips on average. This is **not dollar P/L or a normalized cross-pair return**. The inspected allocation code ranks absolute native-pip predictions across pairs; this can favor large-pip-scale exotics. That ranking and raw-pip aggregation need correction before treating the figure as an economic portfolio comparison. The negative diagnostic is preserved; this audit did not recompute it or translate it into account losses. Direction percentages and eight repeated horizons also do not create eight independent samples per row.

**Feature-forecast v4:** the direction forest blend recorded 2025 out-of-sample mean AUC **0.5215**, balanced accuracy **0.5153**, and failed its development gate. The previously inspected 2026 diagnostic had AUC **0.5216**, balanced accuracy **0.5171**. In contrast, the cost-survival HGB recorded mean development AUC **0.6822**, with diagnostic AUC **0.7020**. That is useful measured structure in whether *either* side's move can survive costs; it does not identify which side should be traded.

The retained two-stage filter obtained **53.76%** development accuracy at **10.97%** coverage and **55.15%** diagnostic accuracy at **6.41%** coverage. Its directional development gate remained false. These are selected-subset rates with explicit coverage, not all-forecast accuracy. The 2026 period was already inspected, so it cannot now become an untouched holdout. The model files are real; their reports explicitly withhold a confirmed-directional/production claim.

**Original227 and corrected220:** the original experiment genuinely reported mean AUC **0.62543** and a passing historical gate. A later retained audit found completed-bar lookahead in the original execution interpretation, full-history volatility metadata, and a path-window bug: nominal 120-minute paths actually spanned **12/24/96 hours** on M30/H1/H4. Those findings invalidate promoting the original score as a correct two-hour execution forecast.

The corrected rebuild discarded seven unsafe metadata fields and rebuilt exact 120-minute M1 paths. The retained best raw corrected-H1 diagnostic had **60 trades, 58.33% win rate, +0.4151 average native pips but −$2.47 under that report's fixed-unit conversion**, and failed mild cost stress. Its development viability gate withheld it; the actually selected no-trade policy therefore has zero trades. The slight positive raw pips and negative dollars illustrate why cross-pair pips are not account profit. The corrected feature/label checks passed, but the diagnostic remained previously inspected. July's statement that no later candles existed is dated; it is not a present-day claim that new evaluation data are unavailable.

**Second-ridge:** all 13 horizon-wide weighted after-cost averages were negative in the retained report. Nevertheless, **87 individual historical profile gates passed**: three at H1, 17 at H2, 30 at H3 and 37 at H4. It would be incorrect to say no historical cell ever passed. They are profile results, share underlying model/price observations and do not establish 87 independent successes. Smoothing improved holdout score in 34/67 fitted pairs; reducing a loss is separate from positive net performance.

**MA643:** one validation cell and a different holdout cell passed; no cell passed both. Root's source-bound M1/H1 example has 2,297 holdout rows over 65 pairs, 49.28% direction, 49.22% balanced accuracy and Brier 0.250027. Only 22.94% of that cell's costs use exact bid/ask observations; the remainder use the documented spread proxy. High magnitude correlations and imbalanced short-horizon direction percentages must remain separate from a tradable directional edge.

**Direct panel:** the retained full-history audit reports hypothetical selected-candidate mean net pips of −2.011/−2.629/−3.284/−12.793/−40.226 for M5/M15/M30/H1/H2. Every selection block failed before holdout reporting, so actual research decisions were no-trade. A recent positive H2 slice did not survive the full-history check. This preserves a real implemented curve architecture without claiming its old weights have a demonstrated edge.

## Integrity and reuse limits

The strongest reusable pieces are the completed-bar multitimeframe builder, exact per-horizon endpoint labels, shared-time cross-pair transforms, compact JSON ridge parameter format, adjacent-log-horizon smoothing, and chronological/purged evaluation machinery. The unified source requires exact future timestamps and continuous real M1 paths; it purges training/validation label maturity. The second-ridge trainer purges horizon plus seven seconds and accepts an S5 target from the nominal endpoint through +7 seconds. That tolerance is a different contract from the current ledger's exact original H1 target and must stay explicit.

Three concrete problems prevent simply turning on the old stack:

1. **Source/artifact compatibility:** canonical C lacks `fresh_m1_intrahour/src`; D retains it. The fitted unified artifact and matrix are `v4_native_pip`, whereas inspected D source is `v5_opportunity`. Restoring the files requires a matched runtime/schema and dependency inventory. A stale path or a newer compatible-looking class name is insufficient.
2. **Feature quality:** the parallel input audit observed five `cross__*_15` columns exactly equal to corresponding `cross__*_1` columns over all 205,029 retained v4 rows. Current v5 source has a missing-return fallback; that is an inferred mechanism across a version difference, while the v4 duplicates are directly measured. The 795 registry comprises price/timeframe, cross-sectional and calendar inputs, not causally joined macro/news observations. More columns are not automatically more independent information.
3. **Evaluation semantics:** second-ridge's historical lower-95 bound uses row standard error, not a horizon/event-block dependence adjustment; profiles and labels overlap. Older fit files do not by themselves provide actual live input availability, post-fit issuance, postcommit visibility, later executable entry and original-target outcome receipts. The newer causal ledger machinery can supply these prospectively while retaining the old results unchanged.

## Reuse recommendation

Recover the existing implementations as **versioned research comparators**, rather than build another broad indicator library from scratch or equate the current H1 study with the entire project. First reconcile the C/D/AppData paths and exact artifact/source schemas; fix duplicated feature semantics and native-pip ranking in a new source version. Select a compact horizon subset supported by actual labels and current input coverage, and publish each valid endpoint with its own availability and target clocks. Do not require every horizon to be present merely to display a valid one.

The existing cost-survival/magnitude models are promising comparison components because their reports show stronger ranking structure than the direction models. That is a reason to test them on later frozen evidence, not an order recommendation. Preserve matched price-only, corrected-feature and news-inclusive comparisons. A genuine price-plus-news curve must add only historically observable news features and demonstrate the effect on held-out, time-blocked outcomes; neither the old795 price surface nor a hand-added news score supplies that proof.

The valuable improvement is therefore **reuse plus repair and prospective measurement**, not erasing the previous work. No model was activated or retrained in this audit.
